# Site Map Template

## Site Purpose

The purpose of the Apple Creek Tree Services website is to introduce the company, explain its tree-care services, show examples of completed work, and make it easy for potential customers to request an estimate or contact the business.

## Primary Audience

Homeowners and property owners in the Apple Creek Tree Services service area who need tree removal, trimming, pruning, stump grinding, storm cleanup, or related tree-care services.

## Primary User Tasks

1. Learn what tree services Apple Creek Tree Services provides.
2. Decide whether the company is a good fit by reviewing company information and completed work.
3. Contact the company or request an estimate for tree service.

## Proposed Site Map

- Home
  - Purpose: Introduce Apple Creek Tree Services, summarize its main services, and guide visitors toward learning more or requesting an estimate.
  - Key content: Company name and logo, hero message, featured services, service-area summary, call-to-action, contact information.

- Services
  - Purpose: Explain the tree-care services offered by Apple Creek Tree Services so visitors can determine which service fits their needs.
  - Key content: Tree removal, trimming and pruning, stump grinding, storm damage cleanup, emergency service information.

- About
  - Purpose: Give visitors background about Apple Creek Tree Services and explain the company’s experience, work approach, and customer-service focus.
  - Key content: Company background, experience, work standards, service philosophy, trust-building information.

- Gallery
  - Purpose: Show examples of completed projects so potential customers can see the type and quality of work performed by Apple Creek Tree Services.
  - Key content: Tree removal photos, trimming and pruning photos, storm cleanup photos, captions, descriptive alt text.

- Contact / Request an Estimate
  - Purpose: Give potential customers a clear way to ask questions, request an estimate, or contact Apple Creek Tree Services directly.
  - Key content: Estimate request form, phone number, email address, business hours, service-area information.

## Content Risks to Resolve

1. Confirm the exact service area, business hours, and emergency-service availability before publication.
2. Collect and approve customer testimonials and project photographs before adding them to the public site.
3. Optimize gallery images, provide descriptive alt text, and test the estimate form and contact links for accessibility and accuracy.
